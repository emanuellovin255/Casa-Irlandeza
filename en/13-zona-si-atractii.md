## Băile Felix and the Apollo thermal pools

Distanța: a 10-minute walk
Poza: vedere-de-pe-balcon-spre-baile-felix.webp
Prima pagină: da

The Apollo pools are a ten-minute walk from the gate — you get there without a car, which
matters more than it sounds when there are four of you and two are children.

Băile Felix is the largest thermal spa resort in Romania. The water comes out of the ground at
41–49 °C and runs all year, so the pools work in January too. The thermal water lily of Peța
grows here as well, a plant that survives in the wild nowhere else in the world.

It is also the treatment centre of the region: rheumatism, post-traumatic conditions,
rehabilitation. People who come for treatment do their course of therapy in the resort and
sleep here, ten minutes away.

## The Nymphaea aquapark, Oradea

Distanța: 7.4 km — a quarter of an hour by car
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The largest indoor aquapark in Romania: slides, a lazy river, a wave pool, a sauna area, and
outdoor pools in summer. It takes a full day and it is the answer to a family holiday in which
it rains.

Fifteen minutes by car, on the road towards Oradea.

---

## Oradea Fortress

Distanța: 6.9 km
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The pentagonal fortress in the centre of Oradea, fully restored, with museums, inner courtyards
and terraces. Entry to the grounds is free; you only pay at the museums.

It is the best starting point for an afternoon in the city: from there you can walk into the
Art Nouveau centre.

---

## The Art Nouveau centre of Oradea

Distanța: about 8 km
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

Oradea has one of the densest concentrations of Art Nouveau architecture in Central Europe: the
Black Eagle Palace with its glass arcade, the Darvas–La Roche House, Unirii Square. Many have
been restored in recent years and can be seen in a two-hour walk.

The restaurants and cafés in the centre are a few steps from one another, so the day closes
itself.

## Băile 1 Mai

Distanța: a few kilometres
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

The neighbouring resort, smaller and quieter than Felix, with the Venus pool and its thermal
basins. Worth it if you want thermal water without the crowds at Felix.

---

## The Pârâul Peța nature reserve

Distanța: a few kilometres
Poza:
Prima pagină: nu

A nature reserve of a few hectares, on a stream that stays warm all year. The thermal water
lily of Peța grows here (Nymphaea lotus var. thermalis), a relic of the Tertiary period that
survives nowhere else in the wild.

It is a short visit, about an hour, and it is the kind of place people do not know exists two
steps from the resort.
