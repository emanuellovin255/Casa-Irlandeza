## Cum ajungi

Titlu: How to find us

Buline: The address is 4A Aurel Vlaicu street, Băile Felix, Bihor county. The map pin is on the building itself
        Băile Felix belongs to the commune of Sânmartin — if a form asks you for the commune, that is it
        By car from Oradea: about 8 kilometres on the road towards Beiuș, a quarter of an hour
        By train: Rontău station is 850 metres away, a ten-minute walk. Băile Felix station is 1.4 kilometres away
        Oradea International Airport is 8 kilometres away. We arrange transfers to and from the airport if you ask in advance
        Parking is in the guesthouse courtyard, free, with room for every car
        The Apollo thermal pools are a ten-minute walk away, and the first restaurants two minutes from the gate
        Check-in between 16:00 and 22:00, check-out between 08:00 and 11:00. Please let us know your arrival time in advance
        We speak Romanian, English, German, French and Italian
