## Povestea noastră

Eticheta: Welcome
Titlu: An Irish house in Băile Felix

The name is simply true: Eoghain is Irish, Florina is from here, and the house belongs to both
of them. Beside the front door there is a blue harp and a small plaque — the marks of Ireland,
put there by people who have reason to.

What comes of that is a three-star guesthouse that behaves like a home. We speak Romanian,
English, German, French and Italian. People who come once tend to come back, usually with
friends, and groups settle in on the terrace and stay there well into the evening.

We are in Băile Felix, on Aurel Vlaicu street. The Apollo thermal pools are a ten-minute walk
away, the Nymphaea aquapark in Oradea a quarter of an hour by car. Two restaurants are two
minutes from the gate, and the shops a five-minute walk.

## Feature-uri alternante

### The hot tub, the sauna and the terrace

Poza: ciubar-cu-hidromasaj-in-aer-liber.webp

On a wooden deck in the courtyard there is a hot tub and a dry sauna. The tub is for the
evening, with the loungers around it; the sauna is for after a full day in the thermal water,
when the body asks for the opposite.

Next to them, the covered terrace: long wooden tables that seat twenty, a bar, a barbecue and a
wood-fired oven. People sit there whatever the weather, because it has a roof.

### The garden, for children and for parents

Poza: gradina-cu-trambulina-si-casuta-de-joaca.webp

The courtyard is enclosed and belongs to the house. There is lawn, fruit trees, a trampoline, a
playhouse, an inflatable slide in summer, and hammocks strung between the trees.

What actually matters is that you can see all of it at a glance. Children can be left to play
while the parents sit on the terrace a few steps away, without getting up at every noise.

### The dining room and the common areas

Poza: sala-de-mese-cu-mese-de-lemn.webp

On the ground floor there is a dining room with wooden tables, a lounge with sofas and a coffee
corner. These are the spaces where a group of twenty can eat together without leaving the house.

They are also the reason groups come here: coach trips, large families, people who return with
their friends. The hosts say themselves that this is what the place is like.
