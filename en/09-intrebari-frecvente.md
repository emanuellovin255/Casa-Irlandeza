# Frequently asked questions — English

<!-- Traducerea lui date/09-intrebari-frecvente.md. -->

## Where exactly are you?

At 4A Aurel Vlaicu street, in Băile Felix, Bihor county. We are inside the resort, not outside
it: the Apollo thermal pools are a ten-minute walk away, and the first two restaurants two
minutes from the gate.

## How late can I arrive?

Check-in is between 16:00 and 22:00, and check-out between 08:00 and 11:00.

Please tell us your arrival time in advance — in your booking, or by phone. It is not a
formality: there is no permanent reception desk, your hosts welcome you in person, so they need
to know when to expect you.

## How far is it to the Apollo thermal pools?

A ten-minute walk. You do not need a car for it.

## How far is the Nymphaea aquapark in Oradea?

7.4 kilometres, roughly a quarter of an hour by car.

## Is there parking?

Yes — private parking in the guesthouse courtyard, free, with room for every car.

## What do the rooms have?

Every room has a balcony, a kitchenette with a dining corner, air conditioning, a flat-screen
satellite TV, a minibar, a private bathroom with shower and hairdryer, linen, towels and
toiletries. Free Wi-Fi throughout the building. The walls are soundproofed.

What differs between the three types is how many people fit and how many square metres they
have: 25 m² for two, 35 m² for three, 46 m² over two levels for four.

## Do you have a hot tub and a sauna?

Yes, both, on the wooden deck in the courtyard. The hot tub has loungers around it, and the
sauna is a dry wooden one. The bar, the barbecue and the wood-fired oven are there too, under
the roof.

## Can I come with children?

Yes — children of any age are welcome, and the garden has a trampoline, a playhouse and
hammocks.

From the age of 6, adult rates apply. An extra bed costs 70 lei per night and a cot 40 lei per
night — please request them when booking; they are paid separately, on site. The family room,
the 46 m² one on two levels, is made for this: the children sleep in the loft.

## Can I bring a pet?

Unfortunately not. Pets are not allowed.

## Is smoking allowed?

No. Smoking is not permitted on the property.

## How do I pay?

In cash, or by bank transfer before arrival — in that case we send you the instructions after
you book.

## How do I book a room?

Press "Check availability", choose your dates and how many of you there are. The button takes
you to our page on Booking.com with the dates already filled in, where you can see what is free
and what it costs.

The standard rate has free cancellation and no prepayment.

## What documents do I need on arrival?

A photo ID and a credit card, for each booking. The minimum check-in age is 18.
